## 🎯 About This Tool

Ai-acct-autopilot helps people who use many AI accounts stay productive. If you reach the usage limits of your account, this tool moves your work to a new account. It keeps your sessions active so your workflow does not stop. You see real-time usage bars directly in your command window. This tool saves you time by removing the manual steps of switching between accounts.

## ⚙️ Setting Up Your Accounts

The tool needs your account details to function. When you open the application, you see a configuration window. You can add your Claude or Codex profile keys here. 

*   Click the "Add Account" button.
*   Copy your API key from the AI service website.
*   Paste your key into the text box.
*   Label the account so you recognize it later.
*   Save your changes. 

Repeat this for every account you own. The tool stores this information securely on your device.

## 📊 Using the Dashboard

The dashboard shows you how much capacity remains for your current account. You see a visual bar that fills up as you use your AI credits. When the bar gets close to the limit, the autopilot kicks in.

The autopilot feature watches your usage. It detects when you approach a rate limit. The tool switches your session to a fresh account without you needing to do anything. Your current task stays active. You do not lose your place in your chat or script.

## 🛠 Features

*   **Auto-Switching:** Moves usage to a new account automatically when nearing limits.
*   **Live Monitoring:** Displays your current credit usage in the command window.
*   **Session Persistence:** Keeps your work moving forward when switching between accounts.
*   **Multi-Platform Support:** Works with both Claude and Codex services.

## 🛡 Security and Privacy

Your keys remain on your computer at all times. The application does not send your private keys to any external servers. The code interacts directly with the AI providers to check your limits and rotate your accounts.

## 🧩 Helpful Commands

You can control the tool using simple commands if you prefer the keyboard:

*   `list`: Shows all your added accounts and their status.
*   `status`: Displays detailed usage information for the active account.
*   `help`: Opens the reference guide for all commands.
*   `exit`: Closes the dashboard and stops the background tasks.

## 📋 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the application does not start, ensure you have the latest drivers for your system. If an account fails, check that your API key is still active on the service website. Sometimes a key expires or gets blocked due to excessive use. You can remove and re-add keys using the dashboard menu if errors persist. 

If your dashboard shows no data, verify your internet connection. The tool needs to reach the AI servers to fetch your current usage bars. You can refresh the dashboard by typing `reload` in the terminal prompt.

*   **Monitoring:** Keep an eye on the dashboard bars during heavy workloads to ensure you have enough active accounts.

Using this tool helps you avoid the frustration of manual account switching. You stay within your limits while keeping your focus on your work. The simplicity of the dashboard makes it easy to track multiple sources of power for your AI projects.
